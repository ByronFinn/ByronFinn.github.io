Not Even Your Life Is Wanted Anymore

https://www.zaobao.com.sg/realtime/china/story20251111-7801146

The news says robots are about to be deployed at scale. The article puts it decently: this is ‘high-quality development,’ this is ‘intelligent upgrading.’ But I keep feeling there is something cold in those words. Like a celebration for a funeral nobody was invited to.

There was a time when, however miserable things got, those at the bottom still had one presentable skill — trading their lives for money. Down the mines, hauling rebar, into the chemical plants. The pay was low, but as long as you were willing to grind, you could always live like someone who was ‘useful.’ Their lives were their last bargaining chip. Now even that chip is being taken over by machines.

AI Uncertainty and Carpe Diem: An Ordinary Person's Thoughts

ByF  published on 2025-10-28 included in  category Deep Thoughts

After reading the recent magazines and reports, my honest advice is this: while these next couple of years are still livable, seize the day and go enjoy yourself.

Two assumptions:

  1. Suppose AI really does keep breaking through and takes over most people’s jobs. Society will inevitably enter a turbulent period of painful adjustment, and nobody knows what will happen.

  2. Suppose AI fails to break through and, in the end, cannot create that much value. Given the enormous leverage the whole world has piled onto AI today, the fallout when the bubble bursts would be no smaller than the 2008 subprime mortgage crisis. Nobody knows what will happen.
